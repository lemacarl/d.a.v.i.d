

# Shepherd King: Game Script
 · 

## Overview
Shepherd King follows David from the sheepfold at Bethlehem to the throne in Jerusalem in four chapters, each one a short run you can win in 10–15 minutes. Every story beat is drawn from 1 and 2 Samuel, and the reference sits beside each scene so the game can show it on screen.

How the roguelike loop carries the story. Each chapter is split into short stages (nights, days or hideouts). Between stages David picks one of three Blessing cards, so every run plays a little differently. If David falls, the run restarts from the chapter's first stage with a short encouragement line; finishing a chapter unlocks the next one for good.
Controls (TV remote only). D-pad moves David. OK uses the main action for the chapter (sling, harp, hide, command). Back pauses. Nothing ever needs two buttons at once.
Playing at three ages.

8-year-old: plays the full game; Normal mode.

4-year-old: Shepherd mode — enemies are slower, sheep wander less, and David cannot fall, only get tired and rest.

2-year-old: Lamb mode — no enemies at all; walk the fields, tap OK to play the harp, gather sheep, hear the narrator. A good "turn" between the older boys' runs.
Tone. Danger is real but never gory: beaten animals run off, soldiers flee or surrender, and defeat is shown with a fade, not a body. Goliath's fall is the one exception the text demands, and the script handles it with a cut-away (Chapter 2).

## Chapter 1: The Shepherd
David guards his father's sheep for five nights, faces a lion and a bear, is anointed by Samuel, and is called to play the harp for King Saul. Main action: OK slings a stone at the nearest predator.

### 1.1 Opening — the hills of Bethlehem
Dawn over green hills. A boy sits on a rock with a harp. Sheep graze.
Narrator: Long ago, in Bethlehem, lived a shepherd boy named David.
Narrator: He was the youngest of eight brothers.
Jesse: David! Keep the sheep safe tonight. Bring them all home.
David: I will, Father. The LORD is my shepherd too!
Caption: "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want." — Psalm 23:1
Tutorial prompts: "Use the arrows to walk." → "Walk near a lost sheep to bring it back." → "Press OK to throw a stone."

### 1.2 Nights 1–3 — the flock
Goal: keep at least 3 of 10 sheep alive until sunrise (about 90 seconds a night). Wolves and jackals creep in from the dark edges; sheep drift toward them. These animals are game additions; Scripture names only the lion and the bear.

Night 1 start — Narrator: The sun goes down. Something moves in the grass.

Night 2 start — David: Stay close, little ones. I'm here.

Night 3 start — Narrator: The wolves are hungry tonight. Be brave, David.

Sheep saved — David: Got you! Back to the flock.

Sunrise — Narrator: Morning comes. The sheep are safe.

### 1.3 Night 4 — the Lion (boss)
A roar. The other animals scatter. A lion grabs a lamb and runs.
David: Hey! That lamb belongs to my father!
The lion runs in circles holding the lamb. Three stone hits make it drop the lamb; three more send it running into the hills.
Narrator: David chased the lion and saved the lamb from its mouth.
Caption: 1 Samuel 17:35

### 1.4 Night 5 — the Bear (boss)
Heavy footsteps. A bear lumbers out of the trees.
David: LORD, help me again!
The bear charges in straight lines; David sidesteps and slings. When it slips on rocks it can be hit twice. Defeat: the bear runs away into the forest.
Narrator: The lion and the bear could not beat the LORD's shepherd.

### 1.5 Cutscene — Samuel comes to Bethlehem
Inside Jesse's house. Seven brothers stand in a row. Samuel walks past each one.
Samuel: Is this one the king? No. Not this one either.
Narrator: Samuel looked at tall Eliab, but God had not chosen him.
Gold light. Caption: "The LORD looketh on the heart." — 1 Samuel 16:7
Samuel: Are these all your sons, Jesse?
Jesse: There is the youngest. He is out with the sheep.
David runs in, out of breath, holding his staff.
Samuel: This is the one.
Samuel pours oil on David's head. Gold sparkles.
Narrator: The Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day on. (1 Sam 16:13)

### 1.6 Bonus stage — the harp for King Saul
A dark palace room. Saul holds his head.
Narrator: King Saul was troubled, and his heart was dark.
Servant: Find a boy who plays the harp well. Jesse's son David!
Rhythm stage, 60 seconds: notes float toward a gold line; press OK as each one crosses it. Each hit brightens the room. Missing never ends the stage, so the 2-year-old can play this one.
Saul (at the end): I feel better. Stay, young David. Stay and play.
Narrator: When David played, Saul was refreshed. (1 Sam 16:23)
Chapter complete: "Chapter 1 complete — The Shepherd. Next: The Hero."

## Chapter 2: The Hero
David carries food to his brothers, gathers five smooth stones, and faces Goliath with one sling. Main action: OK slings; in the boss, David has exactly five stones, and one good throw wins.

### 2.1 Opening — the errand
Narrator: The Philistines came to fight Israel in the valley of Elah.
Jesse: Take this grain and bread to your brothers at the camp. (1 Sam 17:17)
David: Yes, Father! I'll run the whole way.

### 2.2 Stage 1 — the road to the camp
Goal: carry the food basket to the camp in 2 minutes. Dropping the basket (bumping a cart, a goat or a rolling rock) costs one loaf; keep at least one to finish. No enemies here.

Halfway — David: I can see the tents!

Arrival — Narrator: David heard a giant shouting across the valley.

### 2.3 Cutscene — the giant's challenge
Two armies on two hills. A giant in bronze armor walks into the valley.
Goliath: Send me a man! Who will fight me?
The Israelite soldiers step back, afraid.
David: Who is he, to defy the armies of the living God? (1 Sam 17:26)
Eliab: Why are you here? Go back to your little sheep!
David: What have I done now? I only asked a question.

### 2.4 Cutscene — before King Saul
Saul: You are only a boy. He is a warrior.
David: I fought a lion and a bear. God saved me then.
Caption: "The LORD that delivered me out of the paw of the lion… he will deliver me." — 1 Samuel 17:37
Saul puts his armor on David. David wobbles, clanks and falls over.
David: I can't walk in this! I'll take it off.

### 2.5 Stage 2 — the brook
Goal: pick up 5 smooth stones from the stream (glowing stones in the water). Philistine scouts walk along the far bank; if one sees David, he must wait behind a rock until the scout looks away. Every stone collected now is one chance in the boss fight, so a careless player may walk out with fewer than five.

Each stone — David: Smooth and round. Just right.

Five stones — Narrator: He chose five smooth stones from the brook. (1 Sam 17:40)

### 2.6 Boss — Goliath
David walks into the valley with his staff, his sling and his bag.
Goliath: Am I a dog, that you come with sticks? (1 Sam 17:43)
David: You come with a sword and a spear.
David: I come in the name of the LORD of hosts! (1 Sam 17:45)
Phase 1 — the shield-bearer walks in front of Goliath; stones bounce off the shield. Dodge the spear (thrown in straight lines down the D-pad lanes).
Phase 2 — Goliath stops to laugh and tilts his head back; his forehead glows for 2 seconds. Press OK then. A hit ends the fight. A miss uses up a stone.

Goliath laughing: Ha! Ha! A shepherd boy!

Miss — David: Again! The battle is the LORD's.

Out of stones: run ends; restart from 2.5 with "Go back to the brook and try again."

### 2.7 Victory
Slow motion. The stone flies. Cut to the Israelite soldiers' faces: eyes wide. A huge THUD shakes the screen.
Narrator: The stone hit Goliath in the forehead, and he fell on his face. (1 Sam 17:49)
The Philistine army turns and runs. Israel cheers. The script does not show 17:51 on screen; the narrator says only: "David won, and the Philistines ran away." For the 8-year-old, the end-of-chapter card can include the full verse reference so you can read the rest together.
Soldiers: David! David!
Chapter complete: "Chapter 2 complete — The Hero. Next: The Fugitive."

## Chapter 3: The Fugitive
Saul turns against David, and David survives in the wilderness with a growing band of men, twice refusing to harm the king. This is the survival chapter: each hideout is a stage, food and men are the resources, and the lesson is mercy. Main action changes per stage (dodge, hide, sneak, rescue).

### 3.1 Opening — the song that made Saul angry
Women dance in the streets with tambourines.
Women (singing): Saul has slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands! (1 Sam 18:7)
Saul (quietly): They give him more than me.
Narrator: From that day, Saul watched David with jealous eyes.

### 3.2 Stage 1 — the spear in the palace
The harp rhythm stage from Chapter 1 returns, but Saul now throws his spear. Keep playing notes (OK) and step left or right when the spear glows. Three dodges and David escapes through a window.

Saul: I will pin David to the wall! (1 Sam 18:11)

David (escaping): LORD, be my hiding place!

### 3.3 Cutscene — Jonathan's arrows
A field with a big stone. Jonathan arrives with a boy and a bow.
Jonathan: If I shoot past the boy, you must run away.
Interactive: the player watches where the arrow lands. Arrow lands far past the boy:
Jonathan: Is not the arrow beyond you? Hurry! (1 Sam 20:37–38)
David comes out. The two friends hug.
Jonathan: Go in peace. The LORD is between you and me forever. (1 Sam 20:42)

### 3.4 Cutscene — bread and a sword at Nob
David: I am hungry. Do you have any bread?
Ahimelech: Only the holy bread. Take it.
Ahimelech: And here is Goliath's sword, wrapped in cloth. (1 Sam 21:9)
David: There is none like it. Give it to me.
Unlocks the Goliath's Sword Blessing (see Blessings).

### 3.5 Stages 2–4 — the cave of Adullam
Narrator: David hid in a cave. People in trouble came to join him. (1 Sam 22:1–2)
Survival loop, three days. Each day: send men out to gather food and water (OK on a man, then a direction), keep the camp hidden from Saul's patrols, and welcome new followers at the cave mouth. Goal: reach 400 men by the end of day 3. A patrol finding the camp scatters some men.

New follower: I'm in debt and in trouble. Can I follow you?

David: Come. We will trust the LORD together.

Day 3 end — Narrator: About four hundred men followed David.

### 3.6 Stage 5 — the cave at En-gedi
A dark cave. David's men hide deep inside. Saul walks in alone to rest.
David's men (whispering): This is your chance! The LORD gave him to you!
The player sneaks up behind Saul. The only action offered is OK: "cut a corner of his robe". There is no attack button here.
David (later, calling out): My lord the king! I would not harm the LORD's anointed. (1 Sam 24:6, 8–11)
Saul: You are more righteous than I. (1 Sam 24:17)

### 3.7 Cutscene — Abigail
David's men march, angry. A woman rides up on a donkey with bread, raisins and cakes of figs.
Narrator: A rich man named Nabal was rude to David's men.
Abigail: Please, my lord, don't take revenge. Take this gift.
David: Blessed be the LORD who sent you today. (1 Sam 25:32)
After this scene, the next stage starts with extra food.

### 3.8 Stage 6 — Saul's camp at night
Stealth stage. Soldiers lie asleep around the camp; walk only on the dark path. Take the spear and the water jug from beside sleeping Saul, then climb the far hill.
Abishai: Let me strike him once with the spear! (1 Sam 26:8)
David: No. Do not destroy him. (1 Sam 26:9)
David (from the hill): Here is the king's spear! Send someone to get it. (1 Sam 26:22)

### 3.9 Boss stage — the rescue at Ziklag
David's men return home. Smoke rises. The town is burned. Their families are gone.
Narrator: Raiders had taken all the wives and children away.
Narrator: But David encouraged himself in the LORD his God. (1 Sam 30:6)
David: Shall I chase them? Gold light: Pursue, for you will recover all. (1 Sam 30:8)
Rescue stage, 3 minutes: the raiders' camp is spread over a wide field. Untie families (stand next to them and press OK); raiders chase David but run away when hit by a stone. Every family freed adds a follower who helps for the rest of the stage.
Narrator (end): David got back everything. Nothing was missing. (1 Sam 30:19)
Chapter complete: "Chapter 3 complete — The Fugitive. Next: The King."

## Chapter 4: The King
David mourns Saul and Jonathan, becomes king, takes Jerusalem, brings the ark home with dancing, receives God's promise, and shows kindness to Jonathan's son. The chapter ends by pointing forward to the Son of David whose kingdom never ends. Main action: OK commands David's men or acts.

### 4.1 Opening — sad news
Grey sky. A messenger runs up to David.
Narrator: Saul and Jonathan died in a battle on Mount Gilboa.
David: How are the mighty fallen! (2 Sam 1:19)
David: Jonathan, my brother. You were so kind to me.
David plays a slow song on the harp. No input needed; a 20-second quiet moment.

### 4.2 Cutscene — crowned at Hebron
Narrator: The men of Judah anointed David king at Hebron. (2 Sam 2:4)
Narrator: Later, all the tribes of Israel came too. (2 Sam 5:1–3)
Elders: We are your bone and your flesh. You will lead us.

### 4.3 Stage 1 — taking Jerusalem
A city on a hill with high walls. Jebusite guards laugh from the top.
Guards: You'll never get in here! (2 Sam 5:6)
Maze stage: find the hidden water shaft under the city (2 Sam 5:8). Guards on the walls drop rocks onto marked tiles; hidden arrows on the ground point toward the tunnel. Reaching the shaft and climbing it opens the gate for David's men.
Narrator: David took the stronghold of Zion. It became the city of David. (2 Sam 5:7)

### 4.4 Stage 2 — the sound in the treetops
The Philistines gather in the valley of Rephaim.
David: LORD, shall I go up against them?
Gold light: When you hear marching in the treetops, then go. (2 Sam 5:23–24)
Patience stage: the trees sway on screen and the speaker plays wind. Pressing OK too early does nothing but make David's men say "Not yet!" When the treetops rustle with a marching sound, press OK and David's army charges; the Philistines flee. A good stage for the 4-year-old.

### 4.5 Stage 3 — the ark comes home
A joyful parade. Priests carry the ark of the covenant. Trumpets and singing.
Narrator: David danced before the LORD with all his might. (2 Sam 6:14)
The rhythm stage again, now a celebration: arrow prompts (up, down, left, right) make David dance, and every hit adds musicians and dancers to the parade. Misses never end the stage; all three boys can play it.

### 4.6 Cutscene — God's promise
Night. David in his palace. Nathan stands before him.
David: I live in a house of cedar, but God's ark lives in a tent.
Nathan: The LORD says: I will build a house for you.
Gold light. Caption: "Thy throne shall be established for ever." — 2 Samuel 7:16
David: Who am I, O Lord GOD, that you have brought me this far? (2 Sam 7:18)

### 4.7 Final stage — kindness to Mephibosheth
David: Is anyone left from Saul's family? I want to be kind for Jonathan's sake. (2 Sam 9:1)
Gentle search stage: walk through the village of Lo-debar, talk to villagers (OK), and follow their hints to Mephibosheth's house. No enemies.
Mephibosheth: Why would the king be kind to someone like me?
David: Don't be afraid. You will always eat at my table. (2 Sam 9:7)
A long table in the palace. David, his men, Mephibosheth, everyone together.

### 4.8 Ending
Narrator: The shepherd boy became the shepherd of all Israel.
Narrator: And God kept His promise to David.
Narrator: From David's family, God sent a King whose kingdom never ends.
Caption: "The Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David… and of his kingdom there shall be no end." — Luke 1:32–33
Final shot: the hills of Bethlehem at night, sheep resting, a star overhead.
Game complete: "The End. Thank you for playing Shepherd King."

## Recurring lines
These lines repeat across chapters. Pick one at random each time so runs don't feel the same.
When David falls (run ends):

"The LORD lifts up those who fall. Try again!" (Ps 145:14)

"Be strong and of good courage. One more time!"

"Even David had hard days. Let's go again."
Encouragement during play (when hearts are low):

"Don't give up, David!"

"The LORD is with you."

"You can do it. Stay close to the flock."
Stage cleared:

"Well done! Pick a blessing."

"Safe for now. What will help you next?"
Run summary screen: "Sheep saved: 8 · Stones thrown: 23 · Blessings: Smooth Stones, Quick Feet."
Pause menu (Back button): "Resume" · "Restart stage" · "Change mode (Lamb / Shepherd / Normal)" · "Quit to title"
Title screen: "Shepherd King — Press OK to begin." A small sheep bounces beside the word OK.
